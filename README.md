# MatchRating

MatchRating is a lightweight match tracking and rating system for you and your friends.

Record matches, keep a shared history, and see how everyone ranks within each group.

## Get started

### 1. Log in for the first time

Open MatchRating and choose one of the available sign-in options:

- Select **Sign in with Google** to continue with Google.
- Or enter your email address and select **Send login link**. Open the link from the same browser where you requested it.

After you authenticate, complete your profile if prompted. You’ll be taken to your home screen.

### 2. Create a group

1. Open **Groups**.
2. Select **Create group**.
3. Enter a group name, such as your club or regular playing group.
4. Optionally add a description, then select **Create group**.

Your new group opens with you as a member. You can create a match from the group whenever you’re ready to play.

### 3. Invite people

1. Open your group and select **Invite members**.
2. Share the invite using **Share invite**, or select **Copy** to send the link yourself.
3. People can also scan the QR code shown on the invite screen.

When someone opens the invite, they can sign in and accept the invitation to join your group.

## What you can do

- Create groups for different clubs, teams, or friend groups.
- Record singles and doubles matches.
- Review match history and results.
- Track group rankings and player performance over time.
- Share a reusable group invite link or QR code.
